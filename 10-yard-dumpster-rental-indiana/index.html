10 Yard Dumpster Rental in Indiana

10 yard dumpster rental Indiana fits small jobs and heavy loads. It is the smallest roll-off box. It is also the right box for concrete and dirt.

Quick answer

A 10-yard dumpster is about 12 to 14 feet long and 8 feet wide. It stands 3.5 to 4 feet high. It holds about 3 pickup loads, or 50 to 70 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 1 to 3 tons. It suits small cleanouts, single rooms, and heavy debris.

How 10 yard dumpster rental Indiana works

Book the box and a delivery day. The driver sets it on your driveway. You load it over your rental days. Then you call for pickup.

A 10-yard box is short and low. It loads easy from the ground. You can toss bags and small items over the side without a ramp.

Delivery needs a clear path. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width. It needs 60 feet of straight space and 25 to 30 feet of clear height.

Dimensions and capacity

The box is about 12 to 14 feet long. It is 8 feet wide. It stands 3.5 to 4 feet high. That is low enough to load by hand.

It holds about 3 pickup truck loads. It holds 50 to 70 standard trash bags. Think of three truck beds full of debris.

It fits most driveways with room to spare. A standard driveway holds the box and still leaves room for a car. Measure your drive before you book.

Weight limit and overage fees

The weight limit usually runs 1 to 3 tons. Our exact limit is confirmed at booking. [TO CONFIRM] Heavy debris hits the limit fast.

Most published overage rates sit between 85 and 155 dollars per ton across Indiana. The Indianapolis live rate is 155 dollars per ton.

Concrete weighs about 150 pounds per cubic foot. A 10-yard box of concrete can pass its limit while it still looks half full. Stop at the weight cap, not at the rim.

Best jobs for a 10-yard dumpster

A small cleanout fits well. One room, a garage corner, or an attic clearout all fit. So does a small bathroom remodel.

A single-layer roofing tear-off fits. A small roof or a shed roof fills one box. One layer off a small roof weighs about 2 to 3 tons at most.

Heavy debris fits best here. Concrete, brick, dirt, asphalt, and tile all go in a 10-yard lowboy. This is the only size that suits a full heavy load.

Jobs a 10-yard dumpster is wrong for

A whole-house cleanout is wrong for this box. Furniture fills it in a day. You would need two or three boxes, and that costs more than one bigger box.

A kitchen remodel is wrong too. Cabinets, counters, and flooring fill a 10-yard box fast. A 20-yard box suits that job better.

A large construction job is wrong for it. Lumber and drywall fill the volume in hours. Use a 20-yard or 30-yard box for a real build.

Driveway and placement

Boards go under the rails on most driveway drops. Boards spread the weight and guard the surface. Ask for boards when you book. [TO CONFIRM]

Pick flat ground for the box. Stay clear of soft lawn, septic lines, and low wires. Mark the spot so the driver can see it.

A driveway spot usually needs no permit in Indiana cities. A street spot almost always needs one. Check with your city Public Works office first.

Frequently asked questions

How many trash bags fit in a 10-yard dumpster?

About 50 to 70 standard trash bags fit. That is about 3 pickup truck loads. Bulky items like furniture take more space than bags do.

Can a 10-yard dumpster hold concrete?

Yes, it is the right box for concrete. Load it only partway, since concrete hits the weight limit fast. A bigger box full of concrete weighs too much to haul.

Will a 10-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

Yes, in most cases. It is about 12 to 14 feet long and 8 feet wide. A standard driveway holds it with room for a car beside it.

How long can I keep a 10-yard dumpster?

A standard rental runs 7 days. Published market ranges put extensions at 15 to 40 dollars per day across Indiana. [TO CONFIRM]

What if a 10-yard dumpster is too small?

Call for a swap or a second box. A swap brings an empty box and hauls the full one away. Ask about the swap charge when you book. [TO CONFIRM]
