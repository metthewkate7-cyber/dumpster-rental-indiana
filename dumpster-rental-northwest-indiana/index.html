Where does Northwest Indiana waste go?

Northwest Indiana waste goes to permitted transfer stations and landfills. The Lake County C&D Site sits at 18501 Clark Street in Lowell. Indiana permit records list it as a construction and demolition site. It takes construction debris. Transfer stations in Gary and Lake Station serve the lakefront cities. Hazardous waste never goes to a landfill. Paint, oil, chemicals, and batteries need a household hazardous waste drop-off. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management bars open dumping and open burning of waste. Landfills must not accept whole tires, lead-acid batteries, or electronics.
