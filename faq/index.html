How much does it cost to rent a dumpster in Indiana?

A 20-yard box runs $550 to $750 in Indiana, per Ultimate Dumpsters, Nov 2026. In Indianapolis, Dumpsters.com verified $425 to $995 on November 10, 2026, with a $676 average. Read the Indiana prices guide for the full breakdown.

What size dumpster do I need?

Small cleanouts fit a 10-yard box. Kitchen remodels and multi-room cleanouts fit a 20-yard box. Whole-house cleanouts fit a 30-yard box. Read what size dumpster do I need for the job table.

Permits, driveways, and rules

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Indiana?

Driveway placement usually needs no permit in Indiana. Street, sidewalk, and alley placement usually needs a city permit. Read the Indiana permit guide for the city by city rules.

Will a dumpster damage my driveway?

A weak driveway can crack or mark under steel rails. Boards under the rails spread the weight and guard the surface. Ask about driveway protection when you book. Read will a dumpster damage my driveway for more tips.

What can I put in a dumpster?

Household debris, wood, drywall, flooring, and yard waste can go in the box. Hazardous waste, liquids, paint, chemicals, whole tires, lead-acid batteries, and electronics cannot. Read what can you put in a dumpster for the full list.

What happens if I overfill a dumpster?

The driver cannot haul a box loaded past its top edge. You must level the load before pickup can happen. Read what happens if you overfill a dumpster for the full rules.

Time, weight, and choices

How long can I keep a dumpster?

Rentals include 7 days as standard. Your exact rental period is set when you book. Read how long can you keep a dumpster for extension rules.

What are dumpster weight limits and overage fees?

Every size has an included weight limit. Heavy debris reaches that limit before the box fills. Overage runs $85 to $155 per ton across Indiana. Read weight limits and overage fees for the full numbers.

Should I rent a dumpster or hire junk removal?

Choose a dumpster for a big job you load over several days. Choose junk removal for a small job you want gone in one visit. Junk removal costs more per load in most cases. Read dumpster versus junk removal to compare them.

What is the difference between a Bagster and a dumpster?

A Bagster is a small store-bought bag you fill by hand. A dumpster is a steel box a truck drops off. A Bagster holds about 3 cubic yards. Read Bagster versus dumpster rental for the full comparison.
