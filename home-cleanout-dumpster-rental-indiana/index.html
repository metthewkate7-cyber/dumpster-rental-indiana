What a cleanout box holds

A cleanout box takes furniture, mattresses, carpet, boxes, clothes, toys, and old household goods. It also takes small appliances without refrigerant.

Some items stay out. Paint, oil, gas, chemicals, and batteries stay out. So do tires, electronics, and appliances with refrigerant still inside. IDEM rules bar whole tires and lead-acid batteries from Indiana landfills.

Mattresses need a check first. Rules vary by market, and some haulers charge extra per mattress. Ask before you load one. [TO CONFIRM]

What size fits a cleanout?

A 10-yard box fits one room or a garage. It holds about 3 pickup loads and 50 to 70 bags. It suits a small clearout, not a full house.

A 20-yard box fits a multi-room cleanout or a small house. It holds about 6 pickup loads and 110 to 130 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 2 to 4 tons.

A 30-yard box fits a whole-house or estate cleanout. It holds about 9 pickup loads and 170 to 190 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 3 to 5 tons. A full house often needs this size.

What size dumpster do I need for a whole-house cleanout?

A 30-yard box fits most whole-house cleanouts. A 20-yard box fits a small house or a multi-room job. Size up when the house is full of furniture.

Can I put furniture in a cleanout dumpster?

Yes, furniture goes in. Break it down first so it packs flat. A king mattress and two rooms of furniture can pass the weight limit, so watch the tons.

Can I put a mattress in a cleanout dumpster?
