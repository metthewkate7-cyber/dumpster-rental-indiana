Price transparency

Your final bill has four parts. The base rental covers delivery, pickup, disposal, and your rental days. The included weight covers a set number of tons. Overage covers weight past that limit. Extra days cover time past your rental period. Published market ranges show overage fees of $85 to $155 per ton across Indiana. Extra days run $15 to $40 per day across Indiana. Those are market ranges, not our prices. Your exact base price is confirmed on the call before you pay. So are your included tons, overage rate, and extra day rate. Ask for all four numbers. What you need before delivery

Use this checklist before the truck arrives. It keeps delivery fast and safe.

  • Clear a straight path 10.5 feet wide and 60 feet long for the truck.
  • Clear 25 to 30 feet of space above the drop spot, free of wires and branches.
  • Move cars, bins, and toys out of the driveway.
  • Mark the exact drop spot with cones or chalk if the driver needs a guide.
  • Pick driveway placement when you can. A driveway usually needs no permit.
  • Street placement may need a city permit. Get the permit before delivery day.
  • Check your HOA rules before delivery if your home has an HOA.
  • Keep banned items out of the load. See what can go in a dumpster.

If the truck cannot reach the drop spot, delivery may fail. Call us before delivery day if your driveway is narrow, steep, or shared. Read will a dumpster damage my driveway and how to rent a dumpster for more prep tips.
