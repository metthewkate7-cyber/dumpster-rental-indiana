Where does Southern Indiana waste go?

Southern Indiana waste goes to permitted transfer stations and landfills. The Evansville Transfer Station sits at 12900 Warrick County Line Road in Evansville. Indiana permit records show it takes municipal solid waste and construction waste. The Clark-Floyd Landfill sits on State Road 60 in Borden. It is a permitted landfill that takes municipal waste and construction waste. Hazardous waste never goes to a landfill. Paint, oil, chemicals, and batteries need a household hazardous waste drop-off. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management bars open dumping and open burning of waste. Landfills must not accept whole tires, lead-acid batteries, or electronics. Ask us if an item is unclear. We will point you to the right drop-off.
