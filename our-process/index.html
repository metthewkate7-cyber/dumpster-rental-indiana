Our process in six steps

Step 1: Book online

Pick a size on the booking page. Choose your delivery date and rental length. Add your street address and city. Tell us where the box should go: driveway, street, or jobsite. No payment is taken on the booking page.

Step 2: We confirm

We call you to confirm your size and dates. We confirm your exact price before you pay. No payment is taken until the price is confirmed. Tell us about narrow driveways, soft ground, or HOA rules on the call.

Step 3: We deliver

The driver drops the box on your driveway. The truck needs 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight space. It needs 25 to 30 feet of clear height above. Move cars and bins before the truck comes. You do not need to be home for delivery.

Step 4: You fill

Load below the fill line. The fill line sits about 6 inches below the rim. Keep banned items out of the box. Cover the load when rain or snow is forecast. Most rentals include 7 days.

Step 5: We pick up

Call for pickup at least 24 hours ahead. Pickup usually takes 1 to 3 business days after you call. Schedule at least 48 hours ahead when you can. Keep the area clear and move cars out of the path. You do not need to be home for pickup.

Step 6: Final bill

Your final bill reflects size, weight, days, and placement. Overage runs $85 to $155 per ton across Indiana. Extra days run $15 to $40 per day. Your exact price is confirmed before you pay. The timing of the final bill is not published yet. [TO CONFIRM]
