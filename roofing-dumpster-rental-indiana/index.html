How roofing dumpster rental Indiana works

What a roofing box holds

A roofing box takes asphalt shingles, underlayment, felt, flashing, and nails. It also takes wood decking that comes off with the roof. Keep the load to roofing waste only.

Some items stay out. Paint, solvents, and chemicals stay out. So do tires, batteries, and electronics. IDEM rules bar whole tires and lead-acid batteries from Indiana landfills.

Do not mix household trash into a roofing box. Mixed loads can cost more at the landfill. A clean roofing load is the cheapest load to dump.

What size fits a roofing job?

A 10-yard box fits a small single-layer tear-off. It holds about 3 pickup loads. Its weight limit usually runs 1 to 3 tons. It also fits a garage or shed roof.

A 20-yard box fits most single-layer tear-offs on a full house. It holds about 6 pickup loads and 110 to 130 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 2 to 4 tons.

A 30-yard box fits a two-layer tear-off or a large roof with decking. It holds about 9 pickup loads. Its weight limit usually runs 3 to 5 tons. Two layers can blow a 4-ton limit while the box still looks half full.

Weight limits and overage fees

Shingles are heavy. One layer off a 1,500 to 2,500 square foot roof weighs about 2 to 3 tons. Two layers double that. Decking adds more weight on top.

Overage fees run 85 to 155 dollars per ton across Indiana. The Indianapolis live rate is 155 dollars per ton. Across Indiana, overage runs 85 to 155 dollars per ton, Nov 2026.

Count the layers before you book. A roof that looks like one layer can hide a second one. Peel a test patch first. Size up when you find two layers.

Roofers: keep the tear-off on schedule

Roofers lose a day when the box is wrong. A box that fills by noon stops the tear-off. A box that goes overweight costs extra at pickup. Size by weight, not by looks.

Put one person in charge of the box. That person watches the fill line and keeps trash out. The fill line sits about 6 inches below the rim.

Book the pickup before the last shingle comes off. A full box in the drive blocks the gutter crew. Set the call date when you book the drop.

Homeowners: what to expect on tear-off day

Expect noise, nails, and dust on tear-off day. Move cars away from the house. Cover plants near the eaves. Tell the crew about septic lines and soft lawn spots.

Ask the roofer to run a magnet over the lawn. Nails hide in grass and driveways. A magnet sweep at the end of the job finds most of them.

Boards under the box guard your driveway. A loaded roofing box is heavy. Ask for boards when you book.

Frequently asked questions

What size dumpster do I need for a roofing tear-off?

A 10-yard box fits a small single-layer roof. A 20-yard box fits most full-house single-layer tear-offs. Two layers need a 30-yard box, or two boxes.

How much do roofing shingles weigh?

One layer off a 1,500 to 2,500 square foot roof weighs about 2 to 3 tons. Two layers double that. Decking adds more weight on top of the shingles.

Can I put old gutters in a roofing dumpster?

Yes, metal gutters and flashing can go in with the roofing waste. Keep paint cans, solvents, and household trash out of the box.

How long can I keep a roofing dumpster?

A standard rental runs 7 days. Most tear-offs finish in 1 to 3 days. Extensions usually cost 15 to 40 dollars per day across Indiana.

What if the roof has two layers?

Size up before the job starts. Two layers can blow a 4-ton limit while the box still looks half full. A 30-yard box, or a second 20-yard box, covers most two-layer roofs.
