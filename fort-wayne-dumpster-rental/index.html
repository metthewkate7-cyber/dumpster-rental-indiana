Do you need a permit in Fort Wayne?

A dumpster on your own driveway usually needs no permit. A dumpster in public space needs a Right-of-Way Occupancy and Barricade Permit. Get it from the City of Fort Wayne Division of Public Works. Public space means a street, curb lane, sidewalk, or alley. The Right-of-Way Department sits at Citizens Square, 200 E Berry St, Fort Wayne, IN 46802. Apply a few business days before delivery.

Approved street spots need reflective markings, cones, or barricades. Keep the box clear of fire hydrants, storm inlets, driveways, intersections, bus stops, and ADA ramps. Our Indiana permit guide covers the full rule.
