Residential dumpster rental for Indiana homes

Residential dumpster rental gives Indiana homeowners one box for a whole project. You load it at your own pace. We haul it away when you call. It fits cleanouts, remodels, moves, and yard work.

Book a size and a delivery day. The driver drops the box on your driveway. You fill it over your rental days. Then you call for pickup. That is the whole job.

Delivery needs a clear path. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight space. It also needs 25 to 30 feet of clear height above. Move cars and bins before the truck comes.

Small jobs fit a 10-yard box. Think of one room, a small bathroom, or a garage corner. A 10-yard box holds about 3 pickup loads. Most home jobs fit a 20-yard box. A kitchen remodel, a multi-room cleanout, or new flooring all fit. It holds about 6 pickup loads. A 30-yard box suits a whole-house cleanout or a big remodel. It holds about 9 pickup loads.

Most home waste fits in a residential box. Old furniture, boxes, carpet, drywall, wood, and flooring all go in. Yard waste fits too when it is clean and free of trash. Some items never go in. Paint, oil, gas, chemicals, and batteries stay out. So do tires, electronics, and appliances with refrigerant still inside. The state of Indiana bars whole tires and lead-acid batteries from landfills. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management, IDEM, sets these rules. Property managers use home boxes for turnovers. One box clears a unit between tenants. It holds old furniture, carpet, and broken fixtures in one load. A 20-yard box covers most two-bedroom turnovers. Book the pickup before the next tenant moves in.

Built for Indiana

Indiana homes, ground, and city rules shape every rental. These five facts guide how we place and price each box.

Freeze-thaw and driveway protection

Rain and snow add real weight to a load. Wet ground ruts fast under steel rails. A loaded 20 yard can weigh over 10,000 pounds. Boards under the rails spread that weight. Photograph your driveway before delivery day. Read will a dumpster damage my driveway before the truck arrives.

Soil and soft ground

Rain softens soil and grass. A dumpster needs firm ground. A lawn spot may rut after rain. A driveway works best in wet weather. Gravel shifts under steel rails too. Pick a flat, level spot for the drop.

Housing eras, old homes to new builds

Old housing stock drives remodel work. New suburbs drive cleanouts and new builds. Old homes bring roof tear-offs and full cleanouts. New builds bring framing waste and packaging. Both fill boxes fast.

Permits in Indianapolis and every county

Street placement needs a city permit. Indianapolis uses a Right-of-Way Use Permit. It comes from the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services. Standard review takes 3 to 5 business days. Fort Wayne and South Bend have their own rules. Check the permit guide for your city.

HOA notes

Many HOAs set their own dumpster rules. Some ban street placement outright. Others want driveway only placement. Many cap driveway time at 7 to 14 days. Check your HOA rules before you book. Share any rule with us when you book.

Permits and what can go in

A dumpster on your own driveway usually needs no permit. A dumpster on a street, sidewalk, alley, or right-of-way usually needs a city permit. This rule holds in every Indiana city source we checked.

In Indianapolis, street placement needs a Right-of-Way Use Permit from the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services. Apply online through the Accela Citizen Access Portal. Standard review takes 3 to 5 business days. In Fort Wayne, street placement needs a Right-of-Way Occupancy and Barricade Permit. The permit comes from the City of Fort Wayne Division of Public Works. In South Bend, the city asks for at least three business days to process a container permit. In Evansville, no Evansville rule was found, so check with the City of Evansville Public Works before street placement. Read the full Indiana permit guide for the city by city rules.

Indiana waste rules come from IDEM, the Indiana Department of Environmental Management. Waste must go to a permitted facility. Open dumping and open burning are illegal in Indiana. Landfills must not accept hazardous waste, whole tires, lead-acid batteries, electronics, or untreated infectious waste. Keep liquids, paint, oil, gasoline, chemicals, asbestos, and appliances with refrigerant still inside out of the box. Frequently asked questions

How much does a dumpster rental cost in Indiana?

Published market ranges put a 20-yard dumpster at $550 to $750 in Indiana, per Ultimate Dumpsters, Nov 2026. Indianapolis ranged from $425 to $995, with an average of $676, per Dumpsters.com on November 10, 2026. Your exact price is confirmed when you book.

What size dumpster do I need for a home cleanout?

A 10-yard dumpster fits a small cleanout or one room. A 20-yard dumpster fits a multi-room cleanout or a kitchen remodel. Use the estimator on this page to check your job.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Indiana?

A dumpster on your own driveway usually needs no permit. A dumpster on a street, sidewalk, or alley usually needs a city permit. Check with your city public works office before street placement.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Seven days are included as standard. Seven days is the most common rental period. Your exact rental period is confirmed when you book.

What can I put in a dumpster?

You can load household debris, wood, drywall, flooring, and yard waste. You cannot load hazardous waste, liquids, paint, chemicals, whole tires, lead-acid batteries, or electronics. Indiana landfills bar these items under IDEM rules.

Will a dumpster damage my driveway?

Steel rails can mark or crack a weak driveway. Plywood boards under the rails spread the weight and help protect the surface. Ask about driveway protection when you book.

Can I put a mattress in a residential dumpster?

Mattress rules vary by market. Some haulers take them, and some charge extra per mattress. Ask before you load one, so no fee surprises you at pickup.

What if I fill the dumpster before my rental ends?

Call for an early pickup, or ask for an empty box swapped in. A swap keeps the job moving. Extra hauls carry their own charge, confirmed at booking.
