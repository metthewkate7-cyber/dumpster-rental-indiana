Do you need a permit in Indianapolis?

A dumpster on your own driveway usually needs no permit. A dumpster on a street, alley, or sidewalk needs a Right-of-Way Use Permit from the City of Indianapolis. The permit comes from the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services. Apply online through the Accela Citizen Access Portal. Standard review takes 3 to 5 business days. Contact the office at row@indy.gov or 317-327-8583.

Plan ahead for a street spot. Apply before your delivery date. Keep the container clear of fire hydrants, intersections, and driveways. Ask your HOA first if you live in a managed community. Many HOAs want driveway-only placement and a set time limit.

Full permit detail sits in our Indiana permit guide.

Where does Indianapolis trash go?

Most Indianapolis waste goes to South Side Landfill at 2561 Kentucky Ave, Indianapolis, IN 46221. It is the only Subtitle D municipal solid waste landfill in the city. That fact comes from the Indiana Department of Environmental Management permitted facilities list from December 2023. It serves Central Indiana, including Beech Grove, Speedway, Avon, Brownsburg, Greenwood, Carmel, Noblesville, and Zionsville.

Some loads go to transfer stations first. The Marion County Citizens Transfer Station sits at 2324 S. Belmont Ave. Hours and rates change, so check before you drive there. Do not haul hazardous waste to a landfill. Paint, oil, chemicals, and batteries need a household hazardous waste drop-off. The Indiana Department of Environmental Management states that open dumping and open burning of waste is illegal in Indiana.
