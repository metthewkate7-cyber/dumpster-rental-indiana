How to rent a dumpster: the seven steps

  1. Pick your size. Match the box to the job and the debris weight, using the size guide on this site. A 10 yard fits a small cleanout. A 20 yard fits a kitchen remodel. A 30 yard fits a whole house cleanout. Heavy debris needs a small lowboy box.
  2. Pick your dates. Choose a delivery day and a rental term of 7 days as standard. The clock starts on delivery day. Mark pickup day on your calendar at booking.
  3. Choose placement. Pick a flat driveway spot. It needs 10.5 feet wide by 60 feet of clearance, plus 25 to 30 feet overhead. Street placement needs a city permit. Tell us the spot when you book.
  4. Book online or call. Book through the booking page or call [PHONE], and get the full fee stack in writing. Ask for base fee, included tons, overage rate, extension rate, and trip fee.
  5. Prepare for delivery day. Move vehicles, unlock gates, check wires, and lay boards under the rails if needed. The truck arrives any time on delivery day. Be ready early.
  6. Load it right. Load flat and heavy items first, keep banned items out, and stay below the fill line. Break down furniture. Cover the load when rain is forecast.
  7. Schedule pickup. Call at least 24 hours before pickup, keep the area clear, and let the driver haul it away. Pickup usually takes 1 to 3 business days after you call.

Step 1: pick the right size

Size is the first choice and the costliest mistake. Too small means a second haul. Too big wastes money on empty space. Use the estimator on the size guide page. Enter your job type and whether the debris is heavy.

Weight decides for heavy jobs. Shingles weigh about 250 pounds per square. Concrete and dirt need a 10 yard lowboy. Volume decides for light jobs. Furniture and boxes fill space without adding much weight.

Step 2 and 3: dates and placement

Book 2 to 3 days ahead in busy seasons. Spring and summer fill fast. Same day delivery may be possible if you call early. Ask about it when you book. [TO CONFIRM]

Placement is a driveway decision first. A driveway spot usually needs no permit. A street spot needs a city permit. Measure the space. The truck needs 10.5 feet wide by 60 feet straight, plus 25 to 30 feet overhead. Low wires and tree branches block delivery.

Check your HOA rules. Some HOAs want driveway only placement. Some cap the rental at 7 to 14 days. Some want pickup by Friday. Share the rules when you book.

Step 4: book and get it in writing

Book online through the booking page, or call [PHONE]. Give your size, dates, address, and placement. Give your debris type. That is all the booking needs.

Get five numbers in writing before you pay. Base fee, included tons, overage rate per ton, extension rate per day, and trip fee. If a firm will not put those numbers in writing, walk away. Step 5 and 6: delivery day and loading

You do not need to be home for delivery. The driver will place the box where you marked it. Mark the spot with cones or chalk if it is not obvious. Photograph the empty dumpster on arrival. Report any debris already inside at once.

Load heavy and flat items first. Fill gaps with light waste. Keep banned items out. Indiana law bars tires, batteries, electronics, liquids, hazardous waste, and asbestos from landfills. Keep the load below the fill line, about 6 inches below the rim.

Step 7: pickup and after

Call for pickup when the job is done. Call at least 24 hours ahead. Keep cars out of the path. Unlock the gate. Pickup usually takes 1 to 3 business days.

If you fill up early, ask about an empty and return swap. The hauler takes the full box and leaves a fresh one. If you need more time, call before the pickup window passes. Extra days run $15 to $40 per day across Indiana.
