Quick answer

A 30-yard dumpster is about 22 feet long and 8 feet wide. It stands 6 feet high. It holds about 9 pickup loads, or 170 to 190 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 3 to 5 tons. It suits whole-house cleanouts, major renovations, and large construction jobs.

How 30 yard dumpster rental Indiana works

Book the box and a delivery day. The driver sets it on your driveway or jobsite. You load it over your rental days. Then you call for pickup.

Delivery needs clear truck access. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight space. It also needs 25 to 30 feet of clear height.

Dimensions and capacity

The box is about 22 feet long. It is 8 feet wide. It stands 6 feet high. That height holds bulky furniture and long boards.

It holds about 9 pickup truck loads. It holds 170 to 190 standard trash bags. Think of nine truck beds full of debris.

It takes the same footprint as a 20-yard box. It is taller, not longer. A driveway that fits a 20-yard box fits a 30-yard box too.

Weight limit and overage fees

The weight limit usually runs 3 to 5 tons. Our exact limit is confirmed at booking. [TO CONFIRM] Bulky waste fills the volume before it hits the weight.

Market overage rates run 85 to 155 dollars per ton across Indiana. The Indianapolis live rate is 155 dollars per ton.

Heavy items still count. Tile, plaster, and wet debris add weight fast. Load heavy items low and keep the load below the rim.

Best jobs for a 30-yard dumpster

A whole-house cleanout fits. Furniture, carpet, boxes, and old belongings fill one box. An estate cleanout often needs this size.

A major renovation fits. A full gut of several rooms makes bulky waste. Lumber, drywall, and flooring all fit with room to spare.

A large construction job fits. Framing, siding, and roofing waste from a big build fill a 30-yard box. A swap keeps the job moving when it fills.

Jobs a 30-yard dumpster is wrong for

Heavy debris is wrong for this box. Concrete, dirt, and brick need a 10-yard lowboy. A 30-yard box full of concrete can weigh over 10 tons.

A small job is wrong for it. One room or a small bathroom wastes most of the space. A 10-yard box costs less and fits better.

A tight site is wrong for it. A tall box needs clear height and a firm spot. Low wires or soft ground make a smaller box the safer pick.

Driveway and placement

Boards go under the rails on most driveway drops. A loaded 30-yard box is heavy. Boards spread the weight and guard the surface.

Pick a flat spot for the box. Stay clear of soft lawn and septic lines. Mark the spot with cones so the driver can see it.

A driveway spot usually needs no permit in Indiana cities. Street placement almost always needs a city permit. Check with your city Public Works office first.

Frequently asked questions

How many trash bags fit in a 30-yard dumpster?

About 170 to 190 standard trash bags fit. That is about 9 pickup truck loads. Bulky furniture takes more space than bags do.

Can a 30-yard dumpster hold a whole-house cleanout?

Yes, it fits most whole-house cleanouts. A full house of furniture and boxes fills one box in most cases. A very full house may need a swap.

Can concrete go in a 30-yard dumpster?

No, not a full load. A 30-yard box full of concrete can weigh over 10 tons. Use a 10-yard lowboy for concrete, brick, and dirt.

How long can I keep a 30-yard dumpster?

A standard rental runs 7 days. Extensions in published market ranges cost 15 to 40 dollars per day across Indiana. [TO CONFIRM]

Is a 30-yard dumpster too tall to load?

It stands about 6 feet high, so use the side door when one is fitted. Load heavy items first and toss light items over the side. [TO CONFIRM]
