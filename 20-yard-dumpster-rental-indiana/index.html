20 Yard Dumpster Rental in Indiana

Quick answer

A 20-yard dumpster is about 22 feet long and 8 feet wide. It stands 4 to 4.5 feet high. It holds about 6 pickup loads, or 110 to 130 bags. Its weight limit usually runs 2 to 4 tons. It suits kitchen remodels, multi-room cleanouts, flooring, and roofing.

How 20 yard dumpster rental Indiana works

Book the box and a delivery day. The driver sets it on your driveway or jobsite. Load it over your rental days. Then call for pickup.

A 20-yard box is the default size for most jobs. When you are unsure, this is the size to pick. It covers more jobs than any other box.

The truck needs a clear path to the drop spot. It needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight space. Clear height must run 25 to 30 feet.

Dimensions and capacity

The box is about 22 feet long. It is 8 feet wide. It stands 4 to 4.5 feet high. That height lets most adults toss debris over the side.

It holds about 6 pickup truck loads. It holds 110 to 130 standard trash bags. Think of six truck beds full of debris.

It fits most driveways. A 22-foot box needs a clear run of driveway. Measure your drive and check for low wires before you book.

Weight limit and overage fees

The weight limit usually runs 2 to 4 tons. Our exact limit is confirmed at booking. Shingles, tile, and plaster hit the limit fast.

Overage fees usually run 85 to 155 dollars per ton across Indiana. The Indianapolis live rate is 155 dollars per ton.

Wet debris adds weight. Rain soaks drywall and carpet. Cover the box with a tarp on wet days in Indiana, and load heavy items low.

Best jobs for a 20-yard dumpster

A kitchen remodel fits well. Cabinets, counters, flooring, and drywall all fit in one box. So does a bathroom remodel with tile and fixtures.

A multi-room cleanout fits. Two or three rooms of furniture and boxes fill a 20-yard box. It also fits a garage cleanout with old tools and shelving.

A roofing tear-off fits. One layer off a full house weighs about 2 to 3 tons. A 20-yard box holds it with room to spare in most cases.

Jobs a 20-yard dumpster is wrong for

A whole-house estate cleanout is wrong for this box. A full house of furniture fills it fast. A 30-yard box suits that job better.

A major demolition is wrong for it. Walls, floors, and framing fill a 20-yard box in a day. Use a 30-yard or 40-yard box for demolition.

A full concrete load is wrong for it. Concrete, dirt, and brick need a 10-yard lowboy. A 20-yard box full of concrete weighs too much to haul.

Driveway and placement

Boards go under the rails on most driveway drops. A loaded 20-yard box can weigh over 10,000 pounds. Boards spread that weight and guard the surface.

Pick a flat spot for the box. Stay clear of soft lawn and septic lines. Mark the spot with cones for the driver.

In Indiana cities, a driveway spot usually needs no permit. A street spot almost always needs a city permit. Check with your city Public Works office before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 20-yard dumpster the most popular size?

Yes, it is the most rented size in most markets. It fits kitchen remodels, cleanouts, flooring, and roofing. When you are unsure, it is the safe pick.

How many trash bags fit in a 20-yard dumpster?

About 110 to 130 standard trash bags fit. That is about 6 pickup truck loads. Bulky furniture takes more space than bags do.

Can a 20-yard dumpster hold a roofing tear-off?

Yes, one layer off a full house fits in most cases. One layer weighs about 2 to 3 tons. Two layers need a 30-yard box, or two boxes.

How long can I keep a 20-yard dumpster?

Seven days are included as standard. Market extensions usually cost 15 to 40 dollars per day across Indiana.

What if a 20-yard dumpster is too small?

Call for a swap or a second box. A swap brings an empty box and hauls the full one away. Ask about swap charges when you book.
